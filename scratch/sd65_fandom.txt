| colspan="4" |'''SD65''', entitled '''Mega Deck: New Generation (メガデッキ ニュージェネレーション)''', is a Mega Deck in the '''Contract Saga''' that was released on '''April 29th, 2023'''. 


It is a pre-made deck focusing on '''Ultimates'''. A new generation of Four Demon Lords (四魔卿), the popular [[Evil God]] family Ultimates in the God-King Saga, would gather in the deck. In the lore, they are the successor of the titles of the original Four Demon Lords, and each of them has inherited the weapons of the original one. 

The deck also introduces the first '''Contract Ultimate''' to the game. It will lead a new family, [[Hell Contract]], and focuses on moving your own cores and opposing cores around to gain counters. High demand cards needed to build an Ultimate deck are reprinted as Campaign cards. There is also a "Secret Pack" inside, and one random new card in this set with Parallel Illustrations would be included. 

The set includes 19 + 3 cards. Among them are 9 Commons, 1 Rare, 2 Master Rares, 6 X-Rares, 1 Contract X-Rare, and 3 Campaign Promos. Aside from a 40-card deck + 6 Campaign cards, the set also comes with 50 card sleeves, 30 cores, 1 Soul Core, 1 play sheet, and 1 Secret Pack.
